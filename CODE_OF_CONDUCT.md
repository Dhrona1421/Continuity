# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Continuity community aims to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and community events organized around the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not a person's identity or character.
- Explain disagreements calmly and assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, credentials, or private incident data.
- Help keep security reports and synthetic-versus-real data clearly distinguished.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention directed at another participant without consent.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or non-public security details.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or repeatedly disrupting project spaces.
- Encouraging or carrying out unauthorized activity against real systems.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the project maintainer through [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Dhrona1421). For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other reasonable steps to protect the community. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal information in a report.

## Attribution

This policy is written for the Continuity project and informed by the community standards in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
